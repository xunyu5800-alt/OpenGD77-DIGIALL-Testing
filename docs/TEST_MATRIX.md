## 验收顺序 / Test order

1. 记录机背型号和板型，确认已备份。刷机后核对开机版本、背光、旋钮与按键。
   Record the exact board and backups. After flashing, check boot, version, backlight, knob and keys.
2. 先验证 FM 接收与通联，再用已知的色码、时隙和 TG 验证 DMR。分别记录收、发结果。
   Check FM first, then test DMR with a known color code, slot and talkgroup. Record RX and TX separately.
3. DIGIALL 再测中文输入、APRS、C4FM。C4FM 频偏对照只改一项，同功率、同距离，记录热点 BER 和是否掉锁。
   For DIGIALL, continue with Pinyin, APRS and C4FM. Hold other conditions constant when comparing trim, and record hotspot BER and loss of lock.

公开反馈前请去掉账号、密码、声码器来源文件、合入后的固件、私人坐标及无关录音。
Remove credentials, donor files, merged firmware, private coordinates and unrelated recordings before posting.
