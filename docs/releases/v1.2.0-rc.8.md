# v1.2.0-rc.8 · APRS 日志与数字 GPS / APRS log and digital GPS

面向双段 MD‑UV380／MD‑UV390 的免费非商业真机测试版。

- APRS 有效坐标自动弹出，5 秒后返回原信道或 VFO。接收日志最多保留 16 条。按住 SK2 短按 `#` 收藏或取消，满时替换最旧的未收藏条目。正常关机保存日志。
- 5W C4FM DN 随主屏 PTT 携带实时 GPS。按住 SK2 短按绿键打开信道详情，上下选择「发送GPS」，右键开启、左键关闭。存储信道按 SK2＋绿键保存，VFO 按绿键保存。开关独立保存，默认关闭；从 rc.7 升级后，请在需要共享位置的信道重新开启。
- DMR 支持在对应发射时隙选择 GPS 或全部。数字 GPS 使用 10 秒内的有效 GPS 定位。

| 普通 5W＋GPS | PD8／PC6 | 收发 | 收发及 GPS |

两种 10W 的 C4FM TX 仍待各自板卡验证。普通 5W 若按 PTT 显示「仅接收」，请检查该信道的「信道详情 → 仅接收」和安装的 03 包。已有当前字库和拼音时只刷匹配的 03。首次安装按同一硬件的 01 字库、02 拼音、03 主固件顺序刷入。请先备份码表和本机校准，并按 CPS 的声码器合入流程安装。

## English

- APRS position popups return after five seconds. The 16-entry receive log persists across power cycles. Hold SK2 and tap `#` to protect or unprotect an entry. At capacity, a new entry replaces the oldest unprotected entry.
- On 5W, enable **Channel Details → GPS Tx** to attach a fresh position to C4FM DN voice. Hold SK2 and tap GREEN to save to a memory channel, or tap GREEN for VFO. The independent switch defaults to Off. Re-enable it on any rc.7 channels where you want to share your position.
- Both 10W board profiles support FM and DMR TX/RX and C4FM RX. C4FM TX has yet to be validated for those boards. On 5W, check the channel's **RX Only** setting and the installed Stage 03 profile if PTT is blocked.
- All three profiles include Stage 01 font, Stage 02 pinyin, and Stage 03 main images. Existing resources require only the matching Stage 03. Packages exclude proprietary codec and donor binaries.

Host regressions and build checks cover the software paths. Radio GPS interoperability, RF and BER remain to be tested.
