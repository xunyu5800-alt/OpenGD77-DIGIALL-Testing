# v1.2.0-rc.14 · 频扫提速 / Faster frequency scanning

- 普通 VFO 宽范围扫描中，频率显示最多每 100 ms 更新一次。调谐与绘制分开，FM／DMR 的驻留与信号判停条件沿用原值。反向扫描越过下限时从上限继续。
- 瀑布图 FAST 保留 80 个实测频点，调谐后驻留从 25 ms 减到 15 ms，单行驻留预算从 2.0 秒降到 1.2 秒。FINE 和遇信号停留仍用 160 点、每点 25 ms。绘制历史时接收机已调到下一频点。
- FM VFO 长按 `#` 打开瀑布图，长按 `*` 切换 FAST／FINE，短按 `*` 开关遇信号停留，红键退出。

支持双段 MD-UV380／MD-UV390 的 **5W＋GPS、10W PD8、10W PC6**。已安装当前字库与拼音的电台只刷对应的 `03`。首次安装按 `01 → 02 → 03`。

软件测试验证了扫频时序与范围回绕。建议在相同频段和跨度下对比实际成行时间，并测 FAST 在弱信号、窄信号下的表现。

## English

- Ordinary VFO range scanning refreshes the frequency display at most every 100 ms across wide ranges. FM and DMR keep their dwell and signal detection settings. Reverse scans wrap to the upper limit.
- Waterfall FAST retains 80 measurements. Shorter 15 ms settling lowers the dwell budget from 2.0 to 1.2 seconds per row. FINE and stop-and-listen retain 160 points and 25 ms per point. The receiver tunes to the next frequency before drawing the history.
- In FM VFO, hold `#` to open the waterfall. Hold `*` to toggle FAST/FINE, tap `*` to toggle stop-and-listen, and press RED to exit.

Packages cover dual-band MD-UV380/MD-UV390 **5W+GPS, 10W PD8 and 10W PC6**. Flash the matching Stage 03 if current font and pinyin resources are already installed. For a first install, flash Stages 01 through 03 in order.

Host tests cover sweep timing and range wrap. Compare actual row time at the same span and band, and check FAST with weak and narrow signals on the radio.
